# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful.
- Offer constructive feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting are not welcome.

## Reporting

Contact the project maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled as fairly as possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. They will explain the reason for enforcement when practical.
